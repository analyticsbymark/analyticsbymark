# Cookie Policy

This site uses cookies to improve your experience and to understand how the site is used. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time using the consent banner.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site recognise your device on return visits and remember your preferences.

## Cookies we use

### Google Analytics

**Purpose:** We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this site — which pages are read, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve the content we publish.

**Provider:** Google LLC  
**Data retained:** 26 months  
**Opt out:** You can decline this cookie in the consent banner, or install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Mailchimp

**Purpose:** If you subscribe to The Agentic Layer newsletter, Mailchimp sets cookies to manage your subscription and track email engagement (e.g. whether you opened an email or clicked a link).

**Provider:** The Rocket Science Group LLC (Mailchimp)  
**Data retained:** Per Mailchimp's retention policy  
**Opt out:** You can decline this cookie in the consent banner. Note that declining may affect newsletter signup functionality.  
**Mailchimp privacy policy:** [mailchimp.com/legal/privacy](https://mailchimp.com/legal/privacy/)
