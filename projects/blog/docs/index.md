## Is this for you?

You might find my blog helpful if:

* 🧩 You spend more time fixing fragile, inherited data than actually analysing it. You suspect there's a better way to build something that doesn't break each reporting cycle
* 🤔 You're an insurance professional who can see how AI is reshaping how analytics gets done and you don't want to be the one still waiting to be told what to do with it
* 🔁 You want to see the real process - prompts, mistakes, dead ends and not just the highlights reel
* 🎯 You want to move from technically capable to commercially indispensable — the person whose work others depend on, not the one who is only asked "can you pull me this data real quick"

The insurance industry is typically a late mover in new technology, that's not a criticism :material-arrow-right: that's an opportunity. By starting now, you're already ahead — 
you're avoiding being the stragglers who are watching from the sidelines

## What you get

Reading my blog will help you:

2. Build repeatable workflows that you could hand to someone else tomorrow
3. Produce valuable insights that earn you a seat at the table
